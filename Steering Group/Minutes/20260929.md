### 2. AI policy

Bouwe introduced the increasing need to have an AI policy, similarly to other large open-source projects. Preliminary discussions have already been held with the wider ESMValTool community: [ESMValGroup/Community#311](https://github.com/ESMValGroup/Community/discussions/311)

It was suggested that keeping broadly in line with other large repositories would be a reasonable approach.

The importance of the final code being fully understood and trusted by the contributing developer was reiterated, with the developer responsible for signing the CLA.

   - <strong>Action:</strong> Everyone to raise any relevant institutional concerns either on the GitHub issue or with the Steering Group.
   - <strong>Action:</strong> Bouwe to email the steering group to gather any further input, then to draft a policy for review.


### 3. ESMValTool recipe discoverability

Romain raised that we have a strong and growing recipe collection, but finding the right recipe for a specific scientific question, variable, realm, or dataset is not always easy. Axel noted that this has been a long-standing issue, and that discussion at the workshop might be helpful.

An improvement to the search function was suggested.

   - <strong>Action:</strong> Romain to open an issue to enable community discussion, and to make suggestions for continued discussion at the workshop.


### 4. Evaluation task force within ENES-RI framework

Birgit shared that evaluation is one of the pillars of ENES-RI, and that ESMValTool is the main evaluation package within the space.

Bouwe mentioned the modelling and HPC task forces already extant, including sharing the expectations of the Data Task Force, and alternative evaluation tools potentially in use. He suggested that an Evaluation Task Force might have a more open remit than the ESMValTool Steering Group.

Alistair noted that communication between tools is beneficial, but was cautious about the level of benefit potentially delivered for the further commitment.

   - <strong>Action:</strong> Alistair to develop some terms of reference for an Evaluation Task Force within ENES-RI.
   - <strong>Action:</strong> Birgit and Bouwe to support Alistair in the above.
