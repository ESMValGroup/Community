### 2. Consortium planning and reporting

It was noted that having a planning and reporting framework is beneficial in terms of clarifying expectations of consortium members, and for planning future work around expected resources.

 - <strong>Action:</strong> Alistair (with Birgit if possible) to meet with Julien and Supriyo to further discuss the motivation and principles for a planning and reporting framework.

Questions were posed around the most useful formats for the
 1) long term strategy document,
 2) short-term work plans, and
 3) retrospective on recent activity,

as well as around ideas for the content and frequency of each.

Discussions were held around the practicalities of recording short-term plans when current funding and objectives have already been agreed upon, general interactions between plans and work packages, and about how the long-term strategy document is likely to need revisiting annually to ensure that short-term objectives are still in alignment.

It was agreed that all plans should be the product of the consortium as a whole, rather than (for example) each partner being responsible for one section.

Concerns were raised around balancing clarity and confidence in the resources available with visibility of sensitive information. It was agreed that short-term objectives should only be shared with consortium partners rather than being publicly available, and that information on each member's resource commitments should not be shared.

 - <strong>Action:</strong> Alistair to start looking at what information should be captured in each consortium planning and reporting artifact, and at how any relevant decisions should be documented.
